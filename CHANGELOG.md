# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- Hold OK on an app to move it, hide it or open App info.
- All apps shows every app, from the top bar or the apps row.
- Press Home twice to see your recent apps.
- The TV's own set-up screens are now in All apps only.

## 0.2.0
- Home shows Continue watching: up to 20 items from your other apps, newest first.
- Items with a poster show a poster card, and other items show a wide card.
- Each card shows how far you got and the app it opens in.
- Hold OK or press Menu on an item to open its app or hide it.
- Settings › Home rows turns Continue watching on or off, and chooses its apps.
- Settings › Watch Next check shows app channels and TV channels apart.

## 0.1.1
- The top bar's settings button is now named "Launcher settings", apart from Android's Settings app.

## 0.1.0
- MyTVLauncher is a light home screen for Android TV, with no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
- Home shows every installed app, and OK opens it.
- The top bar opens the launcher's settings and Android settings.
- Settings › Home app opens Android's choice of home app, where the TV has one.
- Settings › Accent colour sets the colour of the item that has focus.
- Settings › Watch Next check shows whether the launcher can read other apps' Watch Next items, and tests the Home button.

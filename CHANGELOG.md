# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- App icons fill round cards.
- Video thumbnails fill their cards, with no bars.
- All apps shows a heart on your favourite apps, the ones on Home.
- Hold OK on an app to add it to or remove it from favourite apps.

## 0.8.2
- The card with focus now fills its glow, which is brighter.
- Progress bars sit inside the card, as in MyTVPlayer.
- Hold OK on a recent app to show its options.
- A tile under the recent apps opens All apps.
- The light card behind dark channel logos is a little darker.

## 0.8.1
- Continue watching cards show the episode and time left, such as "S1E2 22m".
- Channel logos are the same size, clear of the card's edges.
- Dark channel logos show on a light card.
- The card with focus grows larger, with a glowing edge.

## 0.8.0
- Settings › Show notifications adds a bell with the TV's notifications.
- OK on a notification opens it; hold OK to dismiss it.

## 0.7.0
- Pictures from Plex, Plezy and other home servers now show.
- The card with focus grows and glows in the accent colour.
- A TV guide button opens the TV's guide, MyTVPlayer's or MyPlexPlayer's.
- The remote's Guide key on Home opens the same guide.
- Channel logos show whole, not cropped.
- Posters in channel rows are larger.
- Hold OK on an app to open it in the Play Store.
- Left and Right change the speaker wake delay.
- The Watch Next check is gone from Settings.

## 0.6.0
- Settings › Speaker wake can wake a sleeping speaker when the TV wakes.

## 0.5.0
- Settings › Backup & restore saves your Home and choices to a file.
- Restore from that file, or open one with the launcher from a file manager.

## 0.4.1
- Settings › Home rows › Row order sets the order of Home's rows.
- Every app's channels are listed in Settings › Home rows, grouped by app.
- Channel cards are half their old size.
- Rows no longer jump when you move up and down.
- Back on a row goes back to its first card.
- A moved app stays in view as it moves.
- All apps can move apps too.

## 0.4.0
- Apps' home screen channels show as rows below your apps.
- Settings › Home rows turns channel rows on or off, one by one.
- The top bar shows the time, the network and a VPN badge.

## 0.3.0
- Hold OK on an app to move it, hide it or open App info.
- All apps shows every app, from the top bar or the apps row.
- Press Home twice to see your recent apps.
- The TV's own set-up screens are now in All apps only.

## 0.2.0
- Home shows Continue watching: up to 20 items from your other apps, newest first.
- Items with a poster show a poster card, and other items show a wide card.
- Each card shows how far you got and the app it opens in.
- Hold OK or press Menu on an item to open its app or hide it.
- Settings › Home rows turns Continue watching on or off, and chooses its apps.
- Settings › Watch Next check shows app channels and TV channels apart.

## 0.1.1
- The top bar's settings button is now named "Launcher settings", apart from Android's Settings app.

## 0.1.0
- MyTVLauncher is a light home screen for Android TV, with no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
- Home shows every installed app, and OK opens it.
- The top bar opens the launcher's settings and Android settings.
- Settings › Home app opens Android's choice of home app, where the TV has one.
- Settings › Accent colour sets the colour of the item that has focus.
- Settings › Watch Next check shows whether the launcher can read other apps' Watch Next items, and tests the Home button.

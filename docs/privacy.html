MyTVLauncher privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

MyTVLauncher is a home screen (launcher) for Android TV and Google TV. This policy tells you what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: the app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It never sends your information to the developer or to anyone else. Its only internet use is to load the pictures that your other apps give to the TV's home screen.

What the app reads on your device

  • Your installed apps: their names and icons, so that Home and All apps can show them and open them.
  • Continue watching and app channels: the items that your other apps put on the TV's home screen (titles, pictures, progress and episode numbers), from the TV's own list of programmes. The app reads them only when you give it the TV listings permission, and only while those rows are on.
  • Recent apps (optional): which apps you used last, from Android's usage access, to show the recent apps panel when you press Home twice. The app reads this only if you give it usage access.
  • Notifications (optional, off by default): the TV's notifications, to show them in the app's top bar. The app reads them only while Settings › Show notifications is on and you gave it notification access. It keeps them in memory only, and never saves them.
  • The network status: whether the TV is on Wi-Fi or Ethernet, and whether a VPN is connected, for the icons in the top bar.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your choices: the app's settings, the order of your apps, the apps you added to or removed from Home, and the rows and items you hid.
  • A copy of Home's rows: the last Continue watching items and channel rows it read, so that Home shows straight away when the TV starts.

This information stays in the app's private storage on your device. The app does not upload it, and Android's automatic cloud backup does not include it.

Who the app connects to

The app connects only to the servers named by your other apps' home screen items, to load their pictures (posters, thumbnails and channel logos). Some of these servers can be on your own home network, such as a media server. Like any internet connection, these servers receive your device's IP address and the technical details of the request. What they do with them is covered by the privacy policies of the apps that gave the pictures.

The app also sends a request, inside your TV only, to MyTVPlayer and MyPlexPlayer, if they are installed, to ask them to bring their home screen rows up to date. This request holds no information about you.

Backups

Settings › Backup & restore can save your settings and your Home to a file on your device. The app makes the file only when you ask. The file does not include your other apps' items.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no server of its own, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer does not sell or share any information, and does not use any information for tracking.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load the pictures for Continue watching and app channels.
  • Network state: for the network icon and the VPN badge in the top bar.
  • Read TV listings: to show Continue watching and your apps' channels. The app asks for it.
  • Usage access (optional): for the recent apps panel. You give it in Android settings.
  • Notification access (optional): for the notifications in the top bar. You give it in Android settings.
  • Storage (Android 9 only): to save and open backup files, only when you ask.
  • Package visibility: the app finds the apps that can open from a TV or phone home screen, so it can list and open them.

Deleting your information

Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app, deletes everything that it stored. Backup files that you made stay where you saved them until you delete them. Turning off a permission in Android settings stops the app from reading that information.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new version with a new date.
